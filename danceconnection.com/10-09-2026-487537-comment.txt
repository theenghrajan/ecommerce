We have added a new "FAQs" section to the product pages, right below the product description and above "Ask a question". It shows the questions and answers as a dropdown (click a question to see the answer) and also adds FAQ schema, so search engines can read the FAQs.

The FAQs are managed from the Shopify admin for each product, so no code change is needed to add or edit them:
1. Add the questions and answers under Content -> Metaobjects -> FAQ.
2. Open the product and select its FAQs in the "Product FAQs" field.
Products without FAQs do not show the section.

The section is live on the default product page layout, which the Women's Shoe Design Center uses: https://www.danceconnection.com/products/comfort-shoe-design-center
Once the FAQs from the content document in comment #15 are added to this product, they will show on the page.
