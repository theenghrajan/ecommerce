Disable, don't delete

✓ Disable the size

It disappears from the website. Past orders and reports that include it keep working. Easy to switch back on if Diane changes her mind.

✗ Delete the size

It can't be undone. Past orders lose their link to the product, and re-adding it later means setting it up from scratch.

In Magento: Catalog → Products → open the product → Configurations → find the size row → set it to Disabled (or open that size's own product and switch "Enable Product" off). Save, then refresh the product page.
