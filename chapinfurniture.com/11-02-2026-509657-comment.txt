We checked this again in more detail, and we need to correct our earlier note. The 15% is not specific to Liberty Furniture. Out of state shipping uses one sliding scale for all brands, based on the order total:
- Up to $799.99: $179 flat
- $800 to $1,299.99: 20%
- $1,300 to $1,699.99: 19%
- $1,700 to $1,999.99: 18%
- $2,000 to $2,299.99: 17%
- $2,300 to $2,599.99: 16%
- $2,600 and above: 15%
The Liberty loveseat in our example ($3,549.99) fell in the top tier, which is why it showed 15%. We have not changed anything here. If you would like a flat 20% on all orders over $800, please confirm and we will update it. Please note this would increase shipping on all larger orders, for every brand.
